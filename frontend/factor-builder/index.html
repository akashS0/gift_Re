How the factors are built

From your file: EP = 1/PE, BP = 1/PB, SP = 1/(PX per sales), DE and EPS as-is, Dividend_Flag = 1 when the dividend yield is above zero.
From the price database: TMR (12-month return %), Momentum (price change over the quarter), Beta and Alpha against the NIFTY Midcap 100, Zscore (price standardised within its industry).
Price window: the quarter shifted forward two months, so Q1 FY25 (Apr–Jun 2024) uses 1 Jun 2024 to 31 Aug 2024. Prices are split and bonus adjusted.

How scoring works

The same file is converted to factors first, then each feature is power-transformed (Yeo-Johnson, or Box-Cox for EP and SP), standard-scaled across the batch, and fed to the model along with the encoded ticker — the steps from oot_preds. Pred_Prob is the model's probability, Pred_Tag is 1 above the threshold. Stocks the model never saw in training, and rows with missing factors, are left unscored and counted below.
